# Company Case Notes

Use this file to choose which original case to read or cite. For full details, open the original source links in `source-index.md`.

## Sierra

Best for: design partners, outcome pricing, top-of-market enterprise CX, founder credibility, paid proof before launch.

Pattern: six design partners, upfront commitment, deep co-build, outcome-based resolution pricing, then scaling with proof in hand.

## Harvey

Best for: prestige-first markets, B2C2B adoption, buyer-owned demos, domain-expert GTM.

Pattern: sell to individual lawyers on painful work, use their own public/legal artifacts in demos, turn elite firm trust into a cascade.

## Decagon

Best for: hard WTP discovery, first paid pilots, support automation, narrow workflow selection.

Pattern: founders ran 100+ interviews, asked "how much would you pay?", used 4-week pilots and pre-agreed success metrics.

## Gong

Best for: founder-led ICP pivot, trial-close validation, category creation, pilots as conversion engine.

Pattern: founders heard "not in budget", moved from sales ops to budget-owning revenue leaders, then validated via alpha customers.

## Glean

Best for: enterprise knowledge/search, founder-network design partners, adoption-data expansion, paid POCs.

Pattern: start with a deep enterprise-search wedge, prove adoption, then use adoption statistics to drive expansion.

## Abridge

Best for: trust-heavy regulated markets, clinician-led sales, evidence architecture, health-system enterprise proof.

Pattern: physician founder plus named health-system proof built trust before broad commercialization.

## Legora

Best for: legal AI, paid expert interviews, high-status market FOMO, live demos, reliability discipline.

Pattern: founder personally sold early deals, paid lawyers for interviews, generated conference demo pull, and paused sales to fix reliability before scaling.

## Deel

Best for: compliance/payroll, land-and-expand, remote-work timing, low-friction entry into larger HR platform.

Pattern: start with international contractor payroll/compliance, then expand into EOR and broader HR/payroll workflows.

## Wiz

Best for: cloud security, elite buyer access, urgent category timing, fast enterprise scale.

Pattern: sell into urgent cloud-security pain with founder credibility and strong security-buyer pull.

## Ramp

Best for: finance workflows, cost-saving positioning, spend controls, expansion across finance operations.

Pattern: align product value with saved money, then expand from cards/spend into broader finance workflows.

## Moveworks

Best for: IT support automation, measurable ticket deflection, long stealth proof-building.

Pattern: build enough production proof before launch to sell quantified autonomous resolution, not a hypothesis.

## Writer

Best for: enterprise AI writing, full-stack enterprise AI, brand/compliance trust.

Pattern: sell enterprise-grade AI around controlled workflows, compliance, and repeatable business writing use cases.

## Hebbia

Best for: financial research, document synthesis, high-value analyst workflow replacement.

Pattern: narrow around expensive research/document synthesis work before broadening.

## Cognition

Best for: AI software engineering, usage economics, enterprise deployment support.

Pattern: sell autonomous engineering work through concrete output and deployment support, not generic coding assistance.

## Incident.io

Best for: engineering workflow adoption, Slack-native deployment, incident response.

Pattern: embed where engineering work already happens, then expand through workflow dependency.

## Intercom / Fin

Best for: AI customer service over an installed base, product-led expansion from existing support workflows.

Pattern: leverage existing support/customer-service surfaces and price against resolved support work.

## Listen Labs

Best for: AI research automation, replacing agency/qualitative research labor.

Pattern: price against speed and cost of research agencies and human interview analysis.
