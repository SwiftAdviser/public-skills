# Case Metaprinciples

Use this file as the fast selector before loading full company cases. It is not a substitute for the company files; it tells the agent which 1-3 references to open for a specific GTM question.

## How to Use

1. Match the user's product to a buyer, workflow, budget, and risk type.
2. Pick the closest cases below.
3. Open the matching `references/companies/<company>.md` files before giving a serious recommendation.
4. Extract a concrete move: buyer, proof, pilot, pricing anchor, demo input, and expansion path.

## Cross-Case Metaprinciples

- **Workflow specificity beats platform breadth.** The winners start with one painful, measurable workflow, then expand from usage, data, cases, seats, departments, or transactions.
- **Trust is part of the product.** In legal, clinical, finance, security, and enterprise data products, compliance, citations, auditability, access control, and expert GTM are not secondary.
- **Proof must be engineered before scale.** Paid pilots, design partners, baselines, named metrics, and conversion terms are the sales motion, not a separate validation phase.
- **Pricing follows displaced labor or controlled spend.** The strongest anchors are avoided headcount, hours saved, agency/vendor cost removed, resolved cases, transactions, or ACU-like consumption of useful work.
- **Distribution is an accelerant only when it is not the product.** A platform channel, installed base, marketplace, SI partnership, or founder network can compress time, but it does not replace the wedge.
- **Wrong-fit revenue damages the reference base.** The first customers are not just revenue; they are future sales collateral. Bad-fit pilots create churn and destroy trust cascades.
